# ATHENA BAND

**The World's First Predictive Health and Performance System**

> "Don't Track Your Body. Predict Its Future."

## About

ATHENA BAND is a next-generation AI health platform made up of a mobile app and a screenless wearable band, worn around the clock. The band is minimalist by design — no display, no notifications, no distractions — with a single job: capture the body's data as precisely as possible, 24/7. That data streams into the ATHENA AI app, where it's analyzed by artificial intelligence.

The core idea: while other wearables show you your *current* state, ATHENA AI analyzes your data to predict *future* risk — helping prevent injuries, illness, and overtraining before they happen.

## The Device

The band continuously measures:

- Heart rate
- Heart rate variability (HRV)
- Sleep quality
- Skin temperature
- Stress level
- Physical activity
- Body load / strain
- Recovery
- User movement

It runs continuously and can charge without being removed from the wrist.

## Core Features

### AI Injury Prevention
Analyzes millions of data points to warn users of elevated injury risk — knee, ankle, shoulder, elbow, back, and muscle injuries.

> "Over the last 7 days, load increased by 35% while recovery dropped by 20%. Risk of right knee injury is elevated."

### Recovery Intelligence
A daily morning report covering sleep quality, recovery level, body energy, fatigue level, and readiness for training load.

### Smart Hydration System
Estimates dehydration risk from physiological data and warns the user before symptoms appear.

### Nutrition Intelligence
The user photographs their food in the app; AI recognizes the items, estimates calories, calculates macros (protein/fat/carbs), and rates nutritional quality.

### Early Health Detection
Tracks deviations from the user's normal baseline to catch early signs of illness, overtraining, chronic stress, insufficient recovery, or reduced immune activity.

### Digital Twin Technology
Builds a digital model of the user's body to forecast fitness level, recovery speed, injury risk, the impact of current training, and how sleep and nutrition affect the body.

### Personal AI Coach
Generates a daily, individualized plan for training, recovery, hydration, nutrition, and rest — adapting recommendations in real time.

## How ATHENA Differs from Whoop

Whoop shows you your body's current state. ATHENA AI analyzes that data and forecasts future risk, helping prevent injuries, illness, and overload before they occur.
