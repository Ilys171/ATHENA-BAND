ATHENA AI

Seven ways ATHENA looks ahead.

Every signal the band collects feeds a single AI system that doesn't just report what's happening in your body — it forecasts what's about to.

AI Injury Prevention

ATHENA analyzes millions of parameters to flag elevated injury risk across the knee, ankle, shoulder, elbow, back, and major muscle groups — before pain shows up.

"Over the last 7 days, load increased by 35% while recovery dropped by 20%. Risk of right knee injury is elevated."

Recovery Intelligence

A morning report, every morning: sleep quality, recovery level, body energy, fatigue, and your readiness to train — distilled into one clear picture.

Smart Hydration System

ATHENA estimates dehydration risk from your physiological data and warns you before symptoms ever appear.

Nutrition Intelligence

Snap a photo of your meal. AI recognizes the food, estimates calories, calculates protein, fat, and carbs, and scores overall nutritional quality.

Early Health Detection

ATHENA tracks deviations from your personal baseline, catching early signs of illness, overtraining, chronic stress, poor recovery, or reduced immune activity.

Digital Twin Technology

AI builds a living digital model of your body to forecast fitness, recovery speed, injury risk, and how today's training, sleep, and nutrition ripple forward.

Personal AI Coach

A new plan every day for training, recovery, hydration, nutrition, and rest — adapting in real time as your body changes.
